**Introduction**

In this lesson, you'll load three sample datasets that ship with Kibana: eCommerce orders, flight data, and web logs. These datasets provide realistic data for exploring Kibana's visualization and analysis capabilities without needing to configure your own data pipelines first.

**Accessing Kibana and Loading Sample Data**

1. On the Welcome home page, click on Add integrations to start adding sample datasets.

<img width="1022" height="1394" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d5f0f349-ac16-458c-8ac3-3af75a7a4e7f" />

🔍 Why this matters

Kibana's Welcome page appears when your Elasticsearch cluster has no data. In production, you would typically add integrations to ingest real data from sources like web servers, applications, or cloud services. For learning, sample data provides a quick way to explore Kibana's features.

2. In the resulting Integrations page, search for Sample data. Click the Sample data integration to load sample datasets.
